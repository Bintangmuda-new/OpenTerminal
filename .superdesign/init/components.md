# Shared components

`Terminal` is the full client shell. `Workspace` is a persistent 12-column grid. `PocketChart` consumes `/api/pocket/snapshot` at 1-second cadence for Pocket Option DEMO market, tick and M1 candle data. `PocketMonitorWidget` is the reusable active-analysis card powering Signal, Live Quote, Trend, Momentum, Volatility, Support/Resistance, B.O.S, Current Candle and Feed Health. `TopBar` provides market search and runtime state. `Sidebar` launches broker-first widgets.
