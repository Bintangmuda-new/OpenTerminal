# Voice and tone

Interface labels are short, direct, and mostly uppercase: `LIVE CANDLE`, `SIGNAL`, `MOMENTUM`. Explanatory Indonesian copy uses concise sentence case.

Keep existing market abbreviations (`O`, `H`, `L`, `BODY`, `RANGE`) and units (`pip`, `s`). Preserve exact numeric formatting from the WPF code. Use middle dots to separate compact values, not long decorative separators.

Status language must distinguish live data, waiting, stale data, and unavailable data. Do not invent trade signals or optimistic claims. DEMO-only language remains explicit.

No emoji. Unicode arrows are existing market-direction glyphs, not decorative icons.
