# OpenTerminal product

Use the source of truth in `desktop/MainWindow.xaml`, `desktop/MainWindow.xaml.cs` and `desktop/App.xaml` before changes. Keep native WPF; no browser UI.

Tokens: `tokens/colors_and_type.css`.
Visual rules: `brand/style-notes.md`.
Copy rules: `brand/voice-and-tone.md`.

The application uses dense black panels, fine neutral borders, cool gray secondary text, and semantic green/red/amber market data. Consolas is reserved for market data. Match existing interactions; do not introduce new decoration or components for small tweaks.
